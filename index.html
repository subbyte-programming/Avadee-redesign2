A few helpful answers

Questions You May Have

What can I expect in the first session?

We'll get to know one another, discuss what you'd like support with, and answer any questions you have. You set the pace.

How do I know if counseling is right for me?

You don't need to be in crisis to benefit from counseling. If something is affecting your well-being or relationships, support may help.

Do you offer in-person or virtual sessions?

Please reach out to confirm current availability, location, and telehealth options.

What if I have questions about fees or insurance?

Contact the practice and we'll help you understand current rates and discuss insurance questions.
